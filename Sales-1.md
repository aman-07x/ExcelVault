# ExcelVault
## 🛠️ Tools & Skills
- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **DataType:** Structured, transactional sales data (order-level, time-stamped)
- **Data Cleaning & Validation**: checked for duplicates, missing values and calculation consistency
- **PivotTables**: multi-dimensional summaries by month, region, channel, category and product
- **Dashboard Design**: KPI cards and charts for a clear business overview
- **KPI Analysis**: Revenue, Orders, Quantity, Average Order Value
- **Segmentation & Trend Analysis**: seasonality, customer type, discount impact
- **Business Insights**: turned data into actionable recommendations
