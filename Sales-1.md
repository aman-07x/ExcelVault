- **Tool:** Microsoft Excel
- **DataType:** Structured, transactional sales data (order-level, time-stamped)
- **Data Cleaning & Validation**: checked for duplicates, missing values and calculation consistency
- **PivotTables**: multi-dimensional summaries by month, region, channel, category and product
- **Dashboard Design**: KPI cards and charts for a clear business overview
- **KPI Analysis**: Revenue, Orders, Quantity, Average Order Value
- **Segmentation & Trend Analysis**: seasonality, customer type, discount impact

- **Trend Analysis:**
  - **Monthly Revenue:** Revenue moves up and down through the year. The best months are Nov ($274K), Jun ($259K) and Apr ($241K). The weakest are Jul ($151K) and May ($154K). The first half ($1.23M) and second half ($1.22M) are almost equal. *Conclusion: there is no clear growth trend, so sales depend on strong months, which suggests campaigns or seasonal demand.*
  - **Product Category:** Computers bring in 54.9% of revenue, followed by Monitors (21.5%) and Networking (10.6%). Accessories and Office Supplies together are only 3.7%. *Conclusion: sales depend heavily on high-priced products.*
  - **Sales Channel:** Online has the most revenue (32.3%) and the most orders. Corporate Sales has only 17% of orders but 29.3% of revenue, because its average order value ($2,158) is more than double Online's ($1,003). Marketplace is the weakest, with the lowest order value ($839).
  - **Region:** Revenue is fairly even. North leads with 22.9% and West is lowest with 17.2%. *Conclusion: no region is a problem. Growth depends more on product and channel than on region.*

- **Business Insights:**
  - The business has 2,000 orders and $2.45M revenue, with an average order value of about $1,226.
  - The top 5 products (Gaming Laptop, Laptop Air 13, Laptop Pro 14, All-in-One PC and Ultrawide Monitor) bring in 57.7% of total revenue.
  - Returning customers give 68.6% of revenue and have a higher average order value than new customers ($1,284 vs $1,116). *Conclusion: keeping existing customers matters more than finding new ones.*
  - About 33% of orders carry a discount of 10% or more, so discounting is common.
  - *Conclusion: focus on promoting laptops and computers, grow Corporate Sales because it has high-value orders, run campaigns in the weak months (May and Jul), and review discounts so they don't cut into profit.*
