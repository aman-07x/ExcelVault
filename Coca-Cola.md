**-Project summary**

-Built an interactive Excel dashboard from ten years (FY’09 to FY’18) of Coca-Cola’s profit and loss statement. It shows revenue, margin and net income trends, where each revenue dollar goes, and a revenue-to-net-income bridge, all controlled by a year slicer and dropdown.

**Tools & Skills**

- **Tool: Microsoft Excel** (PivotTables, Slicers, Power Query, Data Validation)

- **Data Type:** Structured financial statement data (Simfin P&L, USD millions, FY’09 to FY’18)

- **Data Preparation & Validation:** unpivoted year columns into a clean table, and checked that gross profit, operating income, pre-tax income and net income reconcile in every year

- **PivotTables:** summaries by fiscal year and P&L line item, with calculated fields for margins

- **Dashboard Design:** KPI cards and linked charts (column, line, doughnut, waterfall) controlled by a slicer and dropdown

- **KPI Analysis:** Net Operating Revenue, Gross Margin %, Operating Margin %, Net Income

- **Trend Analysis:**
  - **Revenue:** Net operating revenue grew from $31.0B (FY'09) to a peak of $48.0B (FY'12), then fell every year to $31.9B (FY'18), down about 34% from the peak. *Conclusion: the business shrank sharply after FY'12.*
  - **Margins:** Gross margin stayed steady between 60% and 64%. Operating margin dipped to 19.7% in FY'15, then rose to 27.3% in FY'18, its highest in the ten years. *Conclusion: Coca-Cola became more profitable on each dollar of sales even though sales were falling.*
  - **Cost Structure:** SG&A dropped from $17.7B (FY'12) to $10.3B (FY'18), a fall of 42%, faster than the 34% fall in revenue. SG&A went from 36.9% to 32.4% of revenue. *Conclusion: cost cutting protected profit.*
  - **Tax Rate:** The effective tax rate was around 17-25% in most years, but jumped to 82.5% in FY'17.

- **Business Insights:**
  - Operating income fell only 19% ($10.8B to $8.7B) while revenue fell 34%, so cost control did most of the work in protecting profit.
  - The FY'10 net income spike ($11.8B, net margin 33.6%) came from a one-time $5.2B other income gain, not from normal business.
  - The FY'17 net income crash ($1.3B, net margin 3.5%) came from a tax charge of $5.6B, which was 82.5% of pre-tax income. Operating income that year was a healthy $7.6B.
  - From FY'14, "Other income" turned negative in most years (-$1.1B to -$1.8B), which pulled net income down. Interest expense also rose from $355M to $919M.
  - *Conclusion: Coca-Cola's core business stayed strong and profitable. Net income swings were mostly caused by one-time gains and tax, so operating income and operating margin show the real performance better than net income does.*
